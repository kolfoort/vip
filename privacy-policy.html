What information do we receive?

If you submit an enquiry, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, area of interest and any transaction information you include in your message.

The enquiry form sends the information you enter through FormSubmit to VIP Capital at info@vipcapital.eu. The website does not itself store form entries in a VIP Capital database. FormSubmit processes the submission to deliver it; see FormSubmit’s privacy terms for further details.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Why do we use your information?

  • To answer enquiries and assess potential financing or trade requests.
  • To communicate about a potential or existing business relationship.
  • To protect, maintain and troubleshoot the website.
  • To meet applicable legal and accounting obligations.

For enquiries, we use information to respond to your request and, where relevant, to take steps before entering into a contract or pursue legitimate business interests. We retain information where needed to meet legal obligations.

Who may receive the information?

Enquiry details are accessible to the VIP Capital personnel who handle the request. FormSubmit processes form submissions for delivery, and GitHub may process technical data as the website hosting provider. We do not sell personal data.

How long do we keep it?

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and any resulting business relationship. We may retain relevant records longer where required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Technical hosting logs are managed according to the hosting provider’s retention practices.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. The hosting provider and form service may use technical measures needed to deliver and secure their services; see their privacy terms for details.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, or provide data you supplied in a portable format. To make a request, email info@vipcapital.eu. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

International processing and security

VIP Capital is based in the Netherlands. FormSubmit and GitHub may process information under their own privacy terms, including in other countries. We take reasonable steps to limit access to enquiry correspondence to people who need it to handle the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or data practices change. The latest version and revision date will appear on this page.
